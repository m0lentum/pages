2026.08.02
Characters: Aava, Miriel, Salma, Ivu

My second year on ArtFight, great fun once again! If you don't know what that is, I described it in last year's post. This time I went full traditional, working in ink and gouache.

I've posted everything I've got permission to share here, which is almost but not quite all of them. You can also browse all the works on my profile if you have an ArtFight account.
