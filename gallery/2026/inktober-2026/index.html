Inktober 2026

2026.10.31
Characters: Miriel, Ivu, Aava, Olivia
License: Multiple

My favorite Inktober yet and one of my favorite art projects in general, this one was an absolute blast! My gimmick this time was designing dresses in Dressmaker and drawing them on my characters, while also practicing painting with colored inks and water. These won't be my last work using this process — it's great fun and I love how it looks :)

(Just the even-numbered days again, but with more time and effort put into them than ever.)
