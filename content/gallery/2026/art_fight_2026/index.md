My second year on [ArtFight], great fun once again!
If you don't know what that is, I described it in [last year's post][af2025].
This time I went full traditional, working in ink and gouache.

<!-- more -->

I've posted everything I've got permission to share here,
which is almost but not quite all of them.
You can also browse all the works on [my profile] if you have an ArtFight account.

License for the images is All rights reserved unless specified otherwise.


## Attacks

![Ink drawing of a turquoise-haired woman sitting on a sofa
with a comically large cup of coffee and a TV remote in her hands.
She's wearing a frilly pink dress with a corset, boots with heels,
and fishnet sleeves and stockings.
Drawn with a fineliner ink pen, roughly shaded with hatching.](af_attack_mero.jpg)
"Small coffee" featuring Mero for [vampirictecsie]

![Gouache painting of Willow,
a blonde-haired witch with a freckled face and a prominent nose, gathering a bouquet of flowers.
She's holding one flower up to her nose with a relaxed smile and carrying the rest of them in the crook of her arm.
She's wearing a green dress with large sleeves. The background is an abstract flower shape.](af_attack_willow.jpg)
"Bouquet for the buds" featuring Willow for [Smox]

![Gouache painting of Mirana, a dragon lady in human form
with large curved horns and blue scales on her skin, standing on the deck of a log cabin in a wooded valley.
She's wearing a two-layered blue and brown dress
and leaning on a railing looking towards us, surrounded by grass and trees.
It's a calm day, sunny but not blazing.](af_attack_mirana.jpg)
"Cabin in the valley" featuring Mirana for [KBDraws92].
License: [CC BY-NC-SA]

![Ink drawing of an anthropomorphic lizard man wearing a collared short-sleeve jacket,
looking at the viewer with arms crossed.
A loosely drawn sketch using gray inks applied with a brush for midtones.](af_attack_huntra.jpg)
"Lizard guy doodle" featuring Huntra for [aks]

![Ink painting of a muscular woman with a large flame in place of her head
playing the guitar on a stage surrounded by speakers.
She's making a very exaggerated downstroke, implying she's playing heavy music.
She's wearing a skintight black dress and has tattoos on her arms and legs.](af_attack_lady_inferno.jpg)
"No stage lights necessary" featuring Lady Inferno for [ghostcatte]

![Ink sketch of a long-haired elven man wearing a long robe and boots.
He has a star-shaped tattoo on his chest visible over the robe's neckline.
He's holding a staff and looking to the side with a pondering expression.](af_attack_väinö.jpg)
Väinö for [DigiPotato]

![Halfbody portrait in black and sepia ink, depicting a young woman walking briskly with an urgent expression on her face.
Her bag and ponytail swing in the air behind her. She's wearing a cropped t-shirt and leggings.
In the background we see a brick building and a large clock on a pole.](af_attack_tessa.jpg)
"Late for class" featuring Tessa the Alchemist for [Pixalisk]

![Gouache painting of a purple-skinned tiefling wearing a colorful jester costume
of alternating red and blue patterns decorated with yellow hearts and stars.
She's standing on one hoof with the other leg bent in the air,
arms to the sides for balance, and looking down at the viewer with a wide grin.
The background is nondescriptly dark, decorated with glittery hearts and stars mirroring her costume.](af_attack_bless.jpg)
"Bless up" featuring Blasphemy (aka Bless) for [DaQueen]

![An ink painting; portrait of a young woman wearing heavy red makeup extending from eyeshadow to blush and lipstick.

"Rosy" featuring The Starry-Eyed Poetess for [tiiny]
