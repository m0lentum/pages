## Sleep mode

![Digital line drawing of Miriel the elf lying on the ground with her eyes closed and one hand on a book, seen from above.
She's surrounded mostly by empty space, dotted with flowers.
The picture isn't colored or shaded, but silhouettes are filled in with white.
The rest is transparent.](sleep_mode.png)

An overlay to display on my PocketBook InkPad One e-reader in sleep mode.
Here's how it looks on the device itself:

![The same drawing displayed on a PocketBook InkPad One e-reader that's in sleep mode.
The empty space in the drawing lets the last active screen show through dimmed and slightly blurred.
It's a paper by J. Marsden and M. West with the current page talking about "extended Hamilton-Jacobi theory".](sleep_mode_demo.jpg)

(Note in case somebody wants to do the same:
this is not the actual format used by the device;
see [this forum thread](https://www.mobileread.com/forums/showthread.php?t=355272)
for how to do this on PocketBooks.)
